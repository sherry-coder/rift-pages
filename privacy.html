Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Overview

Rift: Reality Shifting ("Rift", "we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information. It applies to Rift on both iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

Data Collection

Rift does not collect personal data or send the content you create to any server. Everything you create in the app is stored locally on your device. The only information that leaves your device is the purchase information needed to verify Rift Pro, described under Subscriptions and Payments below.

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking identifiers
  • No advertising data
  • No cookies or web tracking

Local Data

All content you create in Rift, including your desired reality scripts, journal entries, sensory anchors, and settings, is stored entirely on your device. This data is not accessible to us or any third party.

Photos

If you add photos within the app (such as profile pictures or visualization images), these are stored locally on your device. Rift does not upload, share, or transmit your photos anywhere.

Microphone

If you choose to record your own subliminal affirmations, Rift asks for permission to use your microphone. Recordings are made and stored only on your device. Rift does not upload, share, or transmit your recordings, and the microphone is only used while you are actively recording.

Notifications and Alarms

Rift may request permission to send local notifications (such as nightly shifting reminders) and, on Android, to schedule exact alarms for the Wake Back To Bed alarm. These are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device. No notification or alarm data is sent to external servers.

Subscriptions and Payments

Rift offers optional purchases ("Rift Pro"): auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. On iPhone, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. On Android, purchases are processed by Google through Google Play Billing. We also use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage access to Pro features on our behalf.

When you open Rift or purchase or restore Rift Pro, a random, pseudonymous identifier generated by RevenueCat and your purchase information (such as the product, the store's purchase receipt or token, and purchase dates) are sent to RevenueCat so we can confirm your purchase is valid and unlock Pro features on your device. RevenueCat may also receive basic technical information needed to process these requests, such as your device's operating system, the app version, and your IP address. This data is used solely to provide Rift Pro and is not used for advertising or profiling.

We do not receive your name, email, Apple ID, Google account, or payment card details. For details on how Apple, Google, and RevenueCat handle this data, see the Apple Privacy Policy, the Google Privacy Policy, and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Other Third-Party Services

Aside from Apple, Google, and RevenueCat (used only for purchases as described above), Rift does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Everything you create in Rift (desired realities, scripts, journal entries, recordings, photos, and settings) stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall Rift, which removes it permanently. We never have a copy of it.

The only data kept off your device is the purchase record RevenueCat stores on our behalf (a random identifier and your purchase details). To have it deleted:

  1. Email noshair.v.munawar@gmail.com with the subject "Rift data deletion".
  2. Include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) or your App Store order ID, so we can find the record.
  3. We delete the record within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Deleting this record does not cancel an active subscription; cancel that in Google Play or the App Store first. Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Rift is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only data that leaves the device is the pseudonymous purchase information described above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at noshair.v.munawar@gmail.com.
